Virtual support groups for women

Monika Hicks, LCSW, leads virtual support groups for women who want professional guidance and the company of others facing similar pressures. Each group has its own topic, a facilitator who keeps the conversation focused, and clear ground rules on confidentiality.

Current and upcoming groups

Groups are currently full. Enrollment for new groups opens January 1, 2027.

To join the interest list, use the form below.

What happens in a group

Groups may include topic discussion, reflection prompts and practical tools. Monika guides the conversation, keeps it respectful and makes sure everyone has room to speak. Some groups are therapeutic and therapist-led; others are educational. You will know the structure before you enroll.

Is a group right for you?

  • You want support without feeling alone in it
  • It helps to hear how other women name similar struggles
  • You want a guided space with structure
  • You will protect other members’ confidentiality

Groups can sit alongside individual therapy.

Hicks Consulting

Hicks Consulting helps our clients heal beyond survival, break unhealthy cycles, and build healthier, more fulfilling lives through virtual therapy, coaching, consulting, and mental health support.

Through therapy, coaching, consulting, groups, and organizational training, Hicks Consulting supports clients of all ethnicities while bringing specialized care for Black women to the center of the practice.

This website is for informational purposes only and does not provide emergency or crisis services. Therapy services are virtual and limited to Tennessee. Coaching is not therapy and is not a substitute for mental health treatment.
