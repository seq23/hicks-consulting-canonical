Workplace mental health training and keynote speaking

Hicks Consulting provides in-person and virtual workplace mental health training for organizations whose staff carry heavy workloads or absorb other people’s stress. Every session is led by Monika Hicks, LCSW, and shaped around your audience, setting and goals.

Signature trainings

Burnout-Proofing the Workplace

Burnout shows up in a team long before anyone resigns. This training helps staff and managers recognize the early signs and change the work habits that drive it.

Audience: all staff; managers and team leads.

Participants will be able to:

  • Identify early signs of burnout in themselves and their team
  • Separate workload problems from individual coping problems
  • Agree on team practices that reduce avoidable strain

Trauma-Informed Leadership

How stress and trauma exposure affect the way people communicate, trust and perform, and what leaders can change in everyday decisions.

Audience: executives, directors and supervisors.

Participants will be able to:

  • Explain how chronic stress and trauma exposure affect behavior at work
  • Recognize leadership habits that build or erode trust
  • Apply trauma-informed principles to feedback, conflict and policy

Managing Emotional Load in High-Responsibility Teams

For teams that make high-stakes decisions or take in other people’s distress every day, such as healthcare, education and social services.

Audience: frontline and care-heavy teams and their supervisors.

Participants will be able to:

  • Name the emotional demands built into their role
  • Use short regulation techniques during and after hard moments
  • Set up team check-ins that catch overload early

Boundaries, Communication, and Workplace Wellbeing

Practical boundary and communication skills for people who say yes to everything and then resent it.

Audience: all staff; new and mid-level managers.

Participants will be able to:

  • Recognize their own boundary patterns at work
  • Use clear language to decline, delegate or renegotiate a request
  • Address tension early, before it becomes conflict

From Compassion Fatigue to Sustainable Service

For people whose work is caring for others. Covers compassion fatigue, secondary stress and burnout through scenarios drawn from participants’ own work.

Audience: healthcare, social services, education, ministry and nonprofit teams.

Participants will be able to:

  • Tell compassion fatigue, secondary traumatic stress and burnout apart
  • Use in-the-moment techniques, including short brain breaks, when overwhelmed
  • Build a personal recovery plan for demanding weeks

The Human Side of Retention

A conversation for senior leaders about why people stay or leave, and how burnout and emotional safety show up in turnover.

Audience: executives, HR leaders and boards.

Participants will be able to:

  • Connect burnout and emotional safety to turnover and engagement
  • Spot early warning signs in their own organization
  • Choose next steps that fit their culture and resources

Who these trainings are for

Hospitals and healthcare systems, schools, nonprofits, faith-based and women-centered organizations, and any team carrying a high emotional load. Audiences can be mixed: frontline staff, managers and executives, men and women together.

Formats

  • Keynote speaking for conferences and events: 60–90 minutes
  • Interactive workshop for one team or department: full-day or half-day trainings
  • Leadership session or retreat segment: 2–3 hours, interactive
  • Multi-session series: available, shaped to the organization’s needs

All trainings are delivered in person or virtually.

Monika also provides mental health consulting: organizational wellness strategy, mental health program development, employee well-being initiatives, and trauma-informed workplace practices.

After you send the inquiry form, Monika follows up to tailor the session to your group.

What your team leaves with

  • Managers who can spot early warning signs and respond
  • A clearer view of what the organization can change, beyond individual self-care

About the speaker

Monika Hicks, LCSW, is a licensed clinical social worker and the owner of Hicks Consulting PLLC. She holds an MSSW from the University of Tennessee Knoxville and a BS in Clinical Psychology from Middle Tennessee State University, and has over a decade of experience as a mental health provider, including 5 years as Manager of Clinical Services in healthcare and 7 years as a Mental Health Consultant in corporate settings. Her clinical work with high-achieving professionals shapes what she teaches: how stress builds in people who keep performing, and what helps.

Hicks Consulting

Hicks Consulting helps our clients heal beyond survival, break unhealthy cycles, and build healthier, more fulfilling lives through virtual therapy, coaching, consulting, and mental health support.

Through therapy, coaching, consulting, groups, and organizational training, Hicks Consulting supports clients of all ethnicities while bringing specialized care for Black women to the center of the practice.

This website is for informational purposes only and does not provide emergency or crisis services. Therapy services are virtual and limited to Tennessee. Coaching is not therapy and is not a substitute for mental health treatment.
