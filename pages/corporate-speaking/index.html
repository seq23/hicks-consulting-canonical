What a session leaves behind

  • Shared language for stress, burnout and boundaries, so people can name what is happening without blame.
  • Techniques staff can use the same day, including short regulation exercises that work in the middle of a shift.
  • Managers who can spot the early warning signs and know what to say first.
  • A clearer view of what the organization can change, beyond telling people to take better care of themselves.

Seven talks, one for your audience

Burnout Prevention and Compassion Fatigue

Keynote or half-day workshop

For care-heavy teams, HR directors and employee wellness leads

Teams learn to read the early signals in themselves and in each other, separate a workload problem from a coping problem, and agree on the practices that remove avoidable strain. Built for people whose work brings them close to other people's distress.

Mental Health Awareness Month keynote

Keynote, 60 to 90 minutes, in person or virtual

For all-staff events and wellness managers planning May programming

A session for the month when everyone talks about mental health and few teams change anything. What to say to a colleague who is struggling, what not to say, and the small practices that hold up after the month ends.

Trauma Informed Leadership

Leadership session, 2 to 3 hours

For leadership development and organizational development directors

Leaders see how chronic stress and repeated exposure change the way people communicate, trust and perform, and leave with specific changes to how they give feedback, handle conflict and set policy.

Stress Management and Emotional Regulation

Half-day workshop

For learning and development managers and wellness directors

Regulation skills people can use in the middle of a shift, not only on a quiet afternoon. Built around the moments your staff actually find hard, and rehearsed until they hold under pressure.

Boundaries and Sustainable Performance

Keynote or half-day workshop

For talent development directors and HR business partners

For teams where saying no is expensive. How to set limits that survive a busy quarter, how managers protect recovery without lowering the standard, and why an unsustainable pace reads as commitment until it does not.

Women's Leadership and Mental Wellness

Keynote or leadership session

For women's employee resource groups and talent development directors

The strain that shows up for women in senior roles, how it stays hidden behind competence, and what makes recovery possible for people who are used to carrying the load for everyone else.

Executive Wellness and Resilience

Leadership session or series

For CHROs, vice presidents of HR and executive development directors

Why strain goes unreported at senior levels, how it shows when it finally does, and how to build recovery into a role that does not slow down. For executives and for the people responsible for them.

Hicks Consulting

Hicks Consulting helps our clients heal beyond survival, break unhealthy cycles, and build healthier, more fulfilling lives through virtual therapy, coaching, consulting, and mental health support.

Through therapy, coaching, consulting, groups, and organizational training, Hicks Consulting supports clients of all ethnicities while bringing specialized care for Black women to the center of the practice.

This website is for informational purposes only and does not provide emergency or crisis services. Therapy services are virtual and limited to Tennessee. Coaching is not therapy and is not a substitute for mental health treatment.
