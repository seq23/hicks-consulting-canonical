Online Therapy in Memphis: How Virtual Sessions Actually Work

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · August 27, 2026

Therapy at Hicks Consulting is delivered entirely online, on a secure HIPAA-compliant platform, to clients located in Tennessee — which includes Memphis and the surrounding Tennessee communities. Sessions run 53 to 60 minutes, most clients start weekly, and a 60-minute session is $165 with a $200 initial intake.

Online therapy, sometimes called teletherapy or virtual therapy, is licensed clinical mental health treatment delivered by video rather than in a physical office, and it is governed by the licensing rules of the state where the client is physically sitting during the session. That last clause is the part that surprises people, and it is the single most useful thing to understand before you start looking.

Where you have to be, not where the therapist is

Therapy services at Hicks Consulting are virtual and limited to Tennessee. What that means practically is that your physical location during the session is what matters, not your mailing address and not where you grew up. A Memphis resident who takes a session from their kitchen is in Tennessee. The same person taking a session from a hotel in another state during a work trip is not, and that session usually has to be rescheduled. It is worth planning around rather than discovering mid-month.

What a session involves

  1. Request the first conversation. Consult requests, discovery calls and booking all run through the practice’s secure booking system. Start at the discovery call page or take the intake quiz if you are not sure which service fits.
  2. Initial intake. A 60-minute appointment at $200, where history, goals and fit are established.
  3. Ongoing sessions. Typically 53 to 60 minutes. Most clients begin weekly to build consistency and momentum, and frequency is adjusted as needs evolve.
  4. The platform. A secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform is used so your information stays protected. You need a device with a camera, a reliable connection, and a private place to sit.
  5. Payment. Private pay, with a superbill available for possible out-of-network reimbursement. HSA, FSA and credit cards are accepted.

What virtual therapy here is not built for

Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services, and online therapy is not the right container for someone in immediate danger. It is also not the same thing as coaching: coaching is non-clinical support for clarity, boundaries and decision-making, and it does not replace mental health treatment. If you are not sure which of the two you are looking for, the comparison guide lays out the difference alongside what each costs.

Setting up a room that works

The practical failures of virtual therapy are almost never clinical. They are a housemate on the other side of a thin door, a laptop propped at an angle that makes eye contact impossible, or a connection that drops in the fifth minute of something hard. Sitting in a parked car is a legitimate choice and a very common one. Headphones do more for privacy than any other single decision. Test the link before the first appointment rather than during it.

What this page recommends

If you are in Memphis and looking for a therapist, decide two things before you book: whether you need clinical therapy or non-clinical coaching, and whether you will reliably be physically in Tennessee at your appointment time each week. Those two answers determine whether this practice fits before cost or scheduling enters the conversation. If both are yes, request a discovery call and bring your out-of-network benefit details with you.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, this page is not the right resource. Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room. See the emergency and crisis notice.
