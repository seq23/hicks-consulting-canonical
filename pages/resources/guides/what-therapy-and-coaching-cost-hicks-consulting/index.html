What Therapy and Coaching Cost at Hicks Consulting

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · August 27, 2026

Sessions at Hicks Consulting are $200 for the initial intake or psychiatric evaluation, $165 for a 60-minute session, and $85 for a 30-minute session. This is a private-pay practice, so insurance is not billed directly, but a superbill is available for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA, FSA and credit cards are accepted. Cancelling with less than 24 hours’ notice incurs a $75 fee.

Private pay means the practice does not bill your insurance company directly; you pay the practice, and if you have out-of-network benefits you can submit a superbill to seek reimbursement yourself. That single arrangement explains almost every other cost question on this page, so it is worth understanding before you look at the numbers.

Sessions typically run 53 to 60 minutes. Most clients begin weekly to build consistency and momentum, and frequency is adjusted as needs change. Rates vary by service and offerings change, so the FAQ is the page to check for current pricing before you budget.

What a month actually looks like

People rarely want a per-session number. They want to know what the next few months cost. Weekly 60-minute sessions at $165 come to four or five sessions in a typical month, and the first month also carries the $200 intake. Every-other-week is roughly half that. Thirty-minute sessions at $85 exist partly so that frequency does not have to be the only lever. Nothing here commits you to a package: there is no minimum number of sessions, and frequency is a clinical conversation rather than a billing one.

Ways to pay

  1. Credit card. Accepted.
  2. HSA or FSA. Accepted, which for many people is the single largest practical difference in what care costs after tax.
  3. Superbill for out-of-network reimbursement. A superbill can be provided for you to submit to your insurer. Whether you are reimbursed, and how much, is between you and your plan — check your out-of-network mental health benefit and your deductible before you assume a number.
  4. Good Faith Estimate. As a private-pay practice, a written estimate of expected charges is available. See the Good Faith Estimate page.

Is the first session free?

Consult requests, discovery calls and service booking all run through the practice’s secure booking system, and the discovery call page is where you request that first conversation. A discovery call is a fit conversation, not a therapy session. It is not a substitute for treatment, and it is not the place to work on the thing you are calling about. If you are searching for a free therapy session because cost is the barrier, the more useful conversation is about frequency, session length, HSA or FSA payment, and out-of-network reimbursement — all four of which change the real number more than a single free hour would.

Why this practice does not bill insurance

Private pay allows for a more personalized and confidential experience. It also means there is no insurance company setting the number of sessions, requiring a diagnosis to authorize care, or holding a record of your treatment. The trade-off is real and worth naming: you carry more of the cost up front. Whether that trade is right for you depends on your out-of-network benefit, your budget, and how much the confidentiality matters to you.

Policies that affect what you pay

  • A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required to cancel or reschedule.
  • Late cancellations and missed sessions are charged $75.
  • Further sessions are not scheduled until the account is current.
  • Therapy and coaching are virtual. Organizational trainings are available in person.
  • Therapy is limited to clients in Tennessee. Coaching is non-clinical and is not limited in the same way.

What this page recommends

Before you book, do three things. Call your insurer and ask what your out-of-network outpatient mental health benefit is and what your deductible is. Check whether you have HSA or FSA funds available this plan year. Then decide on frequency and session length together rather than separately — a 30-minute session every week and a 60-minute session every other week cost roughly the same and are very different experiences. When you have those three answers, book a discovery call and bring them to it.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, this page is not the right resource. Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room. See the emergency and crisis notice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a therapy session cost at Hicks Consulting?

A 60-minute session is $165 and a 30-minute session is $85. The initial intake or psychiatric evaluation is $200 for 60 minutes.

Do you take insurance?

No. Hicks Consulting is a private-pay practice. A superbill can be provided for you to submit to your insurance for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Yes. HSA and FSA payments are accepted, as are credit cards.

What happens if I cancel late?

A minimum of 24 hours’ notice is required. Late cancellations and missed sessions incur a $75 fee, and further sessions are not scheduled until the account is current.

Is there a free therapy session or free consultation?

Consult requests and discovery calls are booked through the practice’s secure booking system. A discovery call is a fit conversation rather than a therapy session. If cost is the barrier, frequency, session length, HSA or FSA payment and out-of-network reimbursement usually change the real number more than a single free session would.
