Memphis-Area Service Area: What Is Available Where You Live

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · September 22, 2026

Therapy at Hicks Consulting is virtual and limited to clients located in Tennessee. Coaching is non-clinical and is not limited the same way. Organizational training is delivered in person for Memphis-area organizations and other approved locations. If you live in Mississippi or Arkansas, that distinction is the whole answer to whether we can work together.

A therapy licence is issued by a single state and permits treatment of clients located in that state, which is why a therapist in Memphis may be unable to see a client fifteen minutes away in Mississippi while easily seeing one four hours away in Nashville. The Memphis metro straddles three states, so this comes up here more than almost anywhere else in the country.

If you are in Mississippi or Arkansas

This is the answer nobody wants to give and everybody deserves early. Living in Olive Branch, Southaven or West Memphis puts you inside the Memphis metro and outside the state where this practice is licensed to provide therapy. Distance is not the issue. State lines are.

Two things are still open to you. Coaching is non-clinical support for boundaries, confidence, burnout recovery, self-trust, decision-making and life transitions, and it is not therapy and does not replace mental health treatment — but it is available regardless of which side of the state line you are on. And if you work for or lead an organization, in-person training and keynote speaking are arranged by location and engagement rather than by licence.

If what you need is clinical therapy and you are in Mississippi or Arkansas, the honest recommendation is to search specifically for a therapist licensed in your state. Our guide to choosing a therapist is written to be useful even when the therapist you choose is not this one.

What in-person means here

Therapy and coaching are offered exclusively virtually. That is a deliberate design choice about flexibility and privacy, not a temporary arrangement. Organizational trainings are the exception: those are delivered in person, for workplaces, nonprofits, schools, healthcare-adjacent teams, women-centered organizations, faith-connected spaces and staff groups. If you are looking for an in-person individual appointment in Memphis, this practice is not the right fit and it is better to know that now.

How to check before you book

  • Ask yourself where you will physically be sitting during appointments, not where you live on paper.
  • If that answer is Tennessee at least most weeks, therapy is available; if it is another state, look at coaching or look elsewhere.
  • If you are enquiring on behalf of an organization, use the organizational training inquiry form rather than the clinical booking path.
  • If you are not sure which service you need, the intake quiz is built for exactly that.

What this page recommends

Sort yourself into one of three groups before you do anything else. If you are physically in Tennessee and want clinical care, book a therapy consult. If you are outside Tennessee and want non-clinical support with boundaries, decisions or burnout, book coaching. If you are outside Tennessee and need clinical treatment, use the choosing-a-therapist guide to find someone licensed where you are — that is the right answer even though it points away from us.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, this page is not the right resource. Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room. See the emergency and crisis notice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see a therapist at Hicks Consulting if I live in Olive Branch, Mississippi?

Not for therapy. Therapy services are virtual and limited to clients located in Tennessee. Coaching is non-clinical and is available regardless of state, and organizational training is arranged by location.

Do you offer in-person therapy in Memphis?

No. Therapy and coaching are offered exclusively virtually. Organizational trainings are available in person.

What determines whether I can be seen — where I live or where I am?

Where you are physically located during the session. A Tennessee resident travelling out of state generally cannot hold a therapy session from another state.

Is coaching available outside Tennessee?

Yes. Coaching is non-clinical support and is not limited the way therapy is. It is not therapy and does not replace mental health treatment.

Do you travel for organizational training?

In-person trainings and keynote speaking are available for Memphis-area organizations and other approved locations. Use the organizational training inquiry form rather than the clinical booking path.
