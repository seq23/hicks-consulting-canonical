Mental Health Training for Memphis Organizations: What It Covers and How to Scope It

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · August 27, 2026

Hicks Consulting delivers in-person mental health trainings and keynote speaking for Memphis-area organizations, covering mental health awareness, burnout and compassion fatigue, boundaries and self-care, and emotional intelligence. Engagements are scoped and quoted individually through the organizational training inquiry form, which is a separate route from the clinical booking path.

Organizational mental health training is education delivered to a team rather than treatment delivered to a person: it builds shared language and practical skills around stress, burnout and boundaries, and it is not therapy for anyone in the room. Keeping that line clear is what makes a session safe to attend and useful to run.

What the sessions cover

  • Mental health awareness. Building shared language around emotional wellness, stress, support, and how mental health actually shows up at work.
  • Burnout and compassion fatigue. Aimed at care-heavy, leadership-heavy and emotionally demanding environments, where the people most affected are usually the ones still functioning.
  • Boundaries and self-care. Moving self-care from a slogan into practical skills for sustainability and healthier communication.
  • Emotional intelligence. Self-awareness, empathy, regulation, and more thoughtful conflict navigation.

The focus is on practical mental health education for real workplaces rather than inspiration alone. Monika Hicks, LCSW brings clinical insight and real-world practicality to how people work, lead, communicate, and care for themselves and each other.

How an engagement is scoped

  1. Submit the inquiry. Use the organizational training inquiry form. Organizational requests should not use the clinical booking path — they are deliberately separate routes.
  2. Say who is in the room. Frontline staff, managers, a mixed group and a leadership team need genuinely different sessions.
  3. Name the pressure. "Our team is exhausted and nobody will say so" is a more useful brief than "we would like a wellness session".
  4. Set the format. A keynote, a workshop, or a series changes what is achievable. A single keynote can shift language; it cannot change a workload pattern.
  5. Receive a quote. Training engagements are quoted individually, because scope, format, audience size and location all move the number. There is no published rate card, and a page that invented one would not help you budget.

What training will not fix

This is worth saying before you book rather than after. Training builds awareness, language and skills. It does not resolve understaffing, it does not substitute for a manager who needs to have a difficult conversation, and it is not clinical care for the individual in the room who is struggling most. The most effective engagements are the ones where leadership already knows what structural thing is wrong and wants the team equipped to talk about it honestly.

Individuals who need clinical support are pointed toward therapy, which at Hicks Consulting is virtual and limited to clients located in Tennessee. That referral path is part of running a training responsibly, not an upsell.

What this page recommends

Before you enquire, write two sentences: who is in the room, and what you would want to be different a month afterwards. Those two sentences are what turns a generic wellness booking into a session that lands, and they are also what makes a quote meaningful rather than a guess. Bring them to the inquiry form.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide organizational training in Memphis?

Yes. In-person trainings and keynote speaking are available for Memphis-area organizations and other approved locations.

What does organizational training cost?

Engagements are scoped and quoted individually through the organizational training inquiry form, because format, audience and location all affect the scope. There is no published rate card for training.

Is this the same as consulting for a healthcare facility?

The trainings are mental health education for teams, including healthcare-adjacent teams. They are not operational, clinical or regulatory consulting for a facility.

Can our small business book a single session?

Yes. Formats range from a single keynote to a workshop or a series. A single session can shift shared language; changing an entrenched workload pattern usually needs more than one.

Should we use the booking link or the inquiry form?

The inquiry form. Organizational training inquiries should not use the clinical booking path — those routes are kept separate on purpose.
