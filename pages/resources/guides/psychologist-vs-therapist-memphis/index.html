Psychologists in Memphis: Do You Need a Psychologist, a Therapist, or a Psychiatrist?

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · February 2, 2027

Most people searching for a psychologist want talk therapy, and several licence types provide it — psychologists, licensed clinical social workers, licensed professional counsellors and marriage and family therapists all do psychotherapy. The difference matters in two specific situations: if you need formal psychological testing, look for a psychologist; if you need medication, you need a prescriber, usually a psychiatrist. Hicks Consulting is a licensed clinical social work practice — it provides psychotherapy, not psychological testing and not prescriptions.

A psychologist typically holds a doctoral degree and in many cases provides formal psychological testing; a therapist is a broader term covering several licence types that all provide psychotherapy. The overlap between them is large, and the two places they genuinely diverge are testing and prescribing.

“Psychologists near me” is usually shorthand rather than a technical request. Most people typing it mean “someone qualified I can talk to”, and if that is what you mean, the field is far wider than the word suggests. But the distinctions are real, and there are two situations where getting them wrong wastes months.

The credential map

Titles and scope vary by state, so treat this as orientation and confirm anything decisive with the licensing board in your own state.

The row that surprises people most is the psychologist row. A doctorate is a serious credential and it does not, in most states, come with prescribing authority. If medication is the question, the search term you want is psychiatrist or psychiatric nurse practitioner.

When you specifically need a psychologist

Formal psychological testing is the clearest case. If someone — a school, a physician, an employer, a court — has asked for a formal assessment, or you are seeking evaluation for something like ADHD or a learning difference, that is testing work and it is where doctoral-level training is directly relevant. Ask specifically whether the psychologist does the type of assessment you need, since testing is itself specialised.

Neuropsychological evaluation after an injury or illness is a related and even more specialised case. Both usually involve a battery of standardised instruments over several hours and produce a written report, which is a different product from weekly therapy.

When the licence letters matter less than people fear

For ongoing talk therapy — anxiety, burnout, boundaries, grief, identity, life transitions, relationship stress — the evidence base does not support choosing on credential letters alone. What predicts outcome far better is the working relationship: whether you trust the person, whether you agree on what you are working on, and whether you keep going.

That is why the consult call is worth more than the profile. Two clinicians with identical letters can be entirely different to sit with, and the letters will not tell you which is which.

A short decision path

  1. Do you need a formal assessment or report? Look for a psychologist who does that specific type of testing.
  2. Is medication part of what you are looking for? You need a prescriber — a psychiatrist or psychiatric nurse practitioner. Many people see a prescriber and a therapist in parallel, and that is a normal arrangement rather than a duplication.
  3. Are you looking for ongoing talk therapy? Any of the licensed therapy roles above can provide it. Filter on fit, focus, availability and cost instead of on the letters.
  4. Is what you need non-clinical — goals, decisions, boundaries, momentum? Coaching may be the better match, and it is explicitly not therapy or a substitute for treatment.
  5. Is this urgent? None of the above is a crisis pathway. Use emergency services.

The Memphis-specific catch

Whichever role you decide you need, confirm the licence covers the state you will physically be in during appointments. The Memphis metro crosses into Mississippi and Arkansas; licences do not. This catches out more people here than almost anywhere, and it is worth resolving before you spend an afternoon reading profiles.

What this page recommends

Decide what you actually need before you filter by title. Testing points to a psychologist; medication points to a psychiatrist or psychiatric nurse practitioner; ongoing talk therapy is provided by psychologists, LCSWs, LPCs and LMFTs alike, so choose on fit and practicalities rather than on letters. Frequently asked questions

Is a psychologist better than a therapist?

Not inherently. Both provide psychotherapy. Psychologists commonly hold a doctoral degree and in many cases provide formal psychological testing, which other therapy licences generally do not. For ongoing talk therapy, the working relationship predicts outcome more reliably than the credential.

Can a psychologist prescribe medication?

Generally no, with narrow exceptions in a small number of states. Prescribing is usually the role of a psychiatrist or another medical prescriber such as a psychiatric nurse practitioner.

Is Monika Hicks a psychologist?

No. Monika Hicks is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker. Hicks Consulting provides psychotherapy and does not provide psychological testing, formal assessments or medication management.

Do you do ADHD testing or formal assessments?

No. Psychological and neuropsychological testing is not offered. Those assessments are typically carried out by a psychologist who specialises in that type of evaluation.
