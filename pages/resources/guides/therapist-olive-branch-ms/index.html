Looking for a Therapist in Olive Branch, MS: What the State Line Changes

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · February 9, 2027

Olive Branch is inside the Memphis metro and outside Tennessee, and for therapy that second fact is the one that counts. A licence permits treating clients physically located in the licensing state, so a Memphis therapist twenty minutes away may be unable to see you while a Jackson therapist three hours away can. If you will be sitting in Mississippi during sessions, look for a Mississippi-licensed therapist. Hicks Consulting provides therapy only to clients located in Tennessee; coaching, which is non-clinical, is not limited that way.

Eligibility for therapy is decided by the state you are physically in at the time of the session, not by where you live, where you work, or how far away the therapist is.

Olive Branch is one of the clearest examples of this problem in the country. It is a fifteen-minute drive from parts of Memphis, shares its economy, its traffic and its news, and sits in a different state for every purpose a licensing board cares about. People here routinely find a therapist who feels local in every meaningful sense and then discover the arrangement is not available to them.

That is worth knowing on day one rather than on day thirty. This guide is written to be useful whether or not the therapist you end up with is this practice — and for clinical therapy in Olive Branch, it usually will not be.

How to find and verify a Mississippi-licensed therapist

If clinical therapy is what you need and Mississippi is where you will be, the search is straightforward once you stop filtering by distance.

  1. Search by state, not by city. With virtual care the whole state is available to you, which is a far larger pool than DeSoto County. Filtering to “Olive Branch” discards most of it for no benefit.
  2. Confirm the licence covers Mississippi. Ask directly: “Are you licensed to treat clients located in Mississippi?” A yes is verifiable — licence numbers are public and searchable through the relevant Mississippi licensing board for the profession in question.
  3. Ask about the format. Some Mississippi practices are virtual-only, some in-person, some hybrid. Decide which you want before you look rather than after.
  4. Sort the money out on the first call. Whether they bill insurance or are private pay, the session fee, whether a superbill is available, and the cancellation policy and fee.
  5. Ask what happens in a crisis. Many practices do not provide crisis cover. Know the answer, and know your local emergency options, before you need them.

What is still open to you from this practice

Two things, and it is worth being precise about both.

Coaching. Coaching at Hicks Consulting is non-clinical support for boundaries, confidence, burnout recovery, self-trust, decision-making and life transitions. It is delivered virtually and is not limited by state the way therapy is. It is emphatically not therapy and does not replace mental health treatment — if what you need is clinical care, coaching is the wrong tool and choosing it because it is available would be a mistake.

Organizational training. If you lead or work for an organization in the Olive Branch or wider Memphis area, in-person mental health training and speaking are arranged by location and engagement rather than by clinical licence. That runs through the organizational training inquiry path rather than the clinical booking one.

If you are unsure which of the two — or neither — fits your situation, the intake quiz is built for that decision.

Why the rule exists, briefly

It can feel arbitrary when the state line runs through your commute. The reasoning is that a licence is a state’s assurance about a clinician: their training was verified, their conduct is subject to a board, and clients treated in that state have somewhere to complain and a standard to hold the clinician to. A therapist treating someone in a state that has not licensed them leaves that person without those protections.

So the rule that keeps you from booking twenty minutes up the road is the same rule that gives you recourse with whoever you do book. It is worth the inconvenience, and it is not something a practice can quietly make an exception for.

One more practical note about travel

The location rule cuts both ways. A Tennessee client travelling out of state generally cannot hold a therapy session from another state, which matters if you travel regularly for work. Raise it early; a therapist who knows about it can plan around it rather than cancelling on the day.

What this page recommends

Decide where you will physically be sitting during appointments, most weeks, and let that answer drive everything else. If it is Mississippi and you need clinical therapy, search across the whole state for a Mississippi-licensed therapist and verify the licence on the first call — that is the right answer even though it points away from this practice. If it is Tennessee, book a therapy consult here. If you are outside Tennessee and what you want is non-clinical support with boundaries, decisions or burnout, coaching is available to you regardless of the state line.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see a therapist at Hicks Consulting if I live in Olive Branch, Mississippi?

Not for therapy, if you will be located in Mississippi during sessions. Therapy is virtual and limited to clients located in Tennessee. Coaching is non-clinical and is available regardless of state.

What if I live in Olive Branch but work in Memphis?

What matters is where you are physically located during the session, not where you live. If sessions would consistently be held from Tennessee, raise it directly on a consultation call rather than assuming it either way.

Why can a therapist three hours away see me when one twenty minutes away cannot?

Because a licence is issued by a state and permits treating clients located in that state. Distance is not the constraint; the state line is.

How do I check that a therapist is licensed in Mississippi?

Ask directly whether they are licensed to treat clients located in Mississippi, and confirm the licence through the relevant Mississippi state licensing board for their profession. Licence records are public.

What if I need help right now?

Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room.
