Therapy, Coaching, or Organizational Training: Which One Do You Actually Need?

Author: Monika Hicks, LCSW · Read bio · August 27, 2026

Therapy is licensed clinical mental health treatment, virtual and limited to Tennessee, at $165 for 60 minutes. Coaching is non-clinical support for clarity, boundaries and decisions, and is not therapy. Groups are shared support. Organizational training is in-person education for teams and is quoted per engagement. If you are in distress, start with therapy.

Therapy is licensed clinical treatment for mental health concerns; coaching is non-clinical support for people who are not in crisis but want structure, clarity and accountability. The difference is not intensity or seriousness — it is whether the work is clinical care. Almost every version of the "which one do I need" question resolves once that distinction is clear.

Therapy or coaching: the honest split

Therapy supports clinical mental health care for people who want a deeper space to work through emotional pain, stress, patterns, relationships, burnout or healing that feels hard to navigate alone. Coaching is non-clinical support for people who want clarity, accountability, reflection and practical help as they move through transitions, decision-making, boundaries, confidence or next steps.

The trap is assuming coaching is the lighter option and therapy is for people who are struggling more. That framing gets people into the wrong container. A high-functioning person in real distress does not need a lighter option; they need clinical care that can hold it. Equally, someone who is genuinely not in crisis but stuck on a decision may find therapy slower than the support they were looking for.

Coaching is not therapy and does not replace mental health treatment. That sentence is a boundary, not a disclaimer.

Individual work or organizational work

The other axis is whether the problem is yours or the system’s. If a whole team is burning out, individual therapy for one person on that team is a good thing that will not change the pattern. Organizational training builds shared language around mental health, burnout, compassion fatigue, boundaries and emotional intelligence, which is a different lever than individual care. Organizational enquiries use the training inquiry form rather than the clinical booking path — those are deliberately separate routes.

What none of these are

None of the four is crisis care. Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services. None of them is medication management; prescribing is a separate medical role. And none of them requires you to have already worked out what is wrong — the intake quiz exists precisely because that is the hardest part.

What this page recommends

Ask one question: is this affecting my mood, my relationships, my daily functioning, or my ability to feel like myself? If yes, therapy is the right starting point, and if you are outside Tennessee that means finding a therapist licensed where you are. If no, and the block is more about clarity, boundaries or a decision, coaching is likely the better fit and costs less time to find out. If the pattern belongs to a team rather than a person, start with training.

If you are in immediate danger or crisis, this page is not the right resource. Hicks Consulting does not provide crisis or emergency services. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room. See the emergency and crisis notice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between therapy and coaching?

Therapy is licensed clinical mental health treatment. Coaching is non-clinical support for clarity, accountability, boundaries and next steps. Coaching is not therapy and does not replace mental health treatment.

Which one costs more?

Therapy has published rates: $200 for the initial intake, $165 for a 60-minute session and $85 for a 30-minute session. Coaching and group rates vary by service and are quoted on inquiry.

Can I do both therapy and coaching?

That is a conversation to have directly, because the right answer depends on what each would be doing for you. Start with a consult and describe what you are hoping each would address.

Do you offer a mental health clinic or in-person services?

Therapy and coaching are offered exclusively virtually. Organizational trainings are available in person. Hicks Consulting does not operate a walk-in clinic and does not provide crisis services.

Is coaching available outside Tennessee?

Yes. Coaching is non-clinical and is not limited the way licensed therapy is. Therapy is limited to clients located in Tennessee.
