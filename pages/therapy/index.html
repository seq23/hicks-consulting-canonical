Virtual therapy in Tennessee for women who carry a lot

Monika Hicks, LCSW, provides virtual therapy in Tennessee by secure video for adults located in the state. Most of her clients are high-achieving Black women who keep everything running for everyone else. Clients of every background are welcome.
Book a Consultation

What therapy can help with

  • Anxiety, overthinking and trouble resting
  • Burnout and emotional exhaustion
  • Depression and low mood
  • Low self-esteem and perfectionism
  • Relationship strain and boundaries
  • Grief and life transitions
  • The pressure of balancing work, family and everyone else’s needs

How Monika works

Monika uses Solution Focused Therapy. Sessions center on what you want to be different and the strengths you already have, so each conversation moves toward a concrete next step. Her style is warm and direct.

If your Christian faith matters to you, it can be part of the work. If you would rather leave it out, it stays out.

When medication may help, Monika can refer you to a prescriber for medication management.

What sessions look like

Sessions last 53 to 60 minutes by secure video. Your first appointment is an intake and assessment, and most clients then meet weekly. Frequency can change as your needs do.

Fees and payment

  • Initial intake and assessment: $200
  • 60-minute virtual therapy session: $165
  • 30-minute virtual therapy session: $85

Hicks Consulting is private pay. A superbill can be provided for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA/FSA and credit cards are accepted. Cancellations need 24 hours’ notice; late cancellations and missed sessions are $75. More in the FAQ.

Therapy, coaching or a group?

Therapy is clinical care. If you are functioning well and want help with a decision or transition, coaching may fit better. Support groups can work alongside individual therapy.

Hicks Consulting

Hicks Consulting helps our clients heal beyond survival, break unhealthy cycles, and build healthier, more fulfilling lives through virtual therapy, coaching, consulting, and mental health support.

Through therapy, coaching, consulting, groups, and organizational training, Hicks Consulting supports clients of all ethnicities while bringing specialized care for Black women to the center of the practice.

This website is for informational purposes only and does not provide emergency or crisis services. Therapy services are virtual and limited to Tennessee. Coaching is not therapy and is not a substitute for mental health treatment.
