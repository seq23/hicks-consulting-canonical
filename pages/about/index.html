Monika Hicks, LCSW

Monika Hicks, LCSW, is a licensed clinical social worker in Tennessee and the owner of Hicks Consulting PLLC. She provides virtual therapy, coaching and support groups for women, and in-person and virtual mental health training for organizations.

“My mission in life is not merely to survive, but to thrive, and to do so with some passion, some compassion, some humor, and some style.” — Maya Angelou

Credentials and experience

  • Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW), independent practice in Tennessee
  • Master of Science in Social Work, University of Tennessee Knoxville, 2017
  • Bachelor of Science in Clinical Psychology, Middle Tennessee State University
  • Over a decade as a mental health provider in various settings
  • 5 years as Manager of Clinical Services in healthcare
  • 7 years as a Mental Health Consultant in corporate settings
  • Clinical approach: Solution Focused Therapy
  • Listed on Psychology Today and Therapy for Black Girls

Who she works with

Monika’s practice centers African American women, especially those who identify as “A Strong Black Woman” and are tired of carrying it alone. Clients of every background are welcome. She works with depression, anxiety, low self-esteem, relationship issues and the strain of balancing many responsibilities, and is affectionately known as a mindset shifter.

Faith and purpose

Monika’s Christian faith shapes her sense of calling to support people through times of transition. In sessions, faith is included only when a client wants it. She is committed to reducing the stigma around mental health and counseling.

Outside the practice

Monika is a wife, mother of two and ministry leader. She enjoys international travel, trying new restaurants, and dancing like no one is watching.

Hicks Consulting

Hicks Consulting helps our clients heal beyond survival, break unhealthy cycles, and build healthier, more fulfilling lives through virtual therapy, coaching, consulting, and mental health support.

Through therapy, coaching, consulting, groups, and organizational training, Hicks Consulting supports clients of all ethnicities while bringing specialized care for Black women to the center of the practice.

This website is for informational purposes only and does not provide emergency or crisis services. Therapy services are virtual and limited to Tennessee. Coaching is not therapy and is not a substitute for mental health treatment.
