Cultural fit

Why look for a Black therapist in Memphis, TN?

Working with a Black woman therapist may matter when race, gender, family roles, professional expectations, faith, caregiving, or the pressure to be “the strong one” are part of what you are carrying. Cultural awareness does not replace individualized care, but it can reduce the feeling that you must explain every layer before the real work can begin.

Hicks Consulting serves clients of all ethnicities while intentionally centering the experiences of Black women and other people who have learned to survive by overfunctioning, minimizing their needs, or staying composed at their own expense.

Before you choose

What should you check before booking any Black therapist in Memphis?

Directory listings make it easy to scroll and hard to compare. Whoever you choose, these checks take a few minutes and save a mismatched first session.

  • Licence and state. Confirm the therapist holds an active Tennessee licence, because a Tennessee licence is what allows care to clients located in Tennessee.
  • Format. Ask whether sessions are in person, online or both. Monika's are online only.
  • What they help with. Look for the concerns you are carrying, such as anxiety, depression-related heaviness, burnout, grief, self-esteem, relationship strain or life transitions.
  • Cost and payment. Get fees in writing before the intake. Hicks Consulting is private pay and lists its fees on this page.
  • Faith and culture. If you want your faith or cultural context in the room, ask directly how the therapist handles it.
  • Crisis plan. Outpatient therapy is not emergency care. Keep the 988 Lifeline in your phone, and SAMHSA's find support guide lists more ways to get help.

Frequently asked questions

Questions people often ask before reaching out.

Is there a Black therapist near me in Memphis, TN?

Yes. Monika Hicks, LCSW is a Black woman therapist who sees eligible clients located in Tennessee, including Memphis, through secure virtual sessions. There is no physical office, so you can meet from home or work.

Is Hicks Consulting only for Black women?

No. Hicks Consulting serves clients of all ethnicities while bringing specialized cultural awareness and intentional support for Black women to the center of the practice.

Does Monika take insurance, and what does therapy cost?

Hicks Consulting is a private-pay practice. The initial intake is $200, a 60-minute session is $165 and a 30-minute session is $85. A superbill can be provided for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards are accepted.

Does culturally aware therapy mean every experience will be assumed?

No. Cultural awareness should make space for context without replacing curiosity. Your individual experiences, values, needs, and goals still guide the work.

Can coaching be used instead of therapy?

Coaching is non-clinical and is not therapy. The most appropriate path depends on what you need and whether clinical treatment is indicated.

How do I book with a Black therapist at Hicks Consulting?

Use the secure SimplePractice consultation link on this page to review availability and request a first conversation with Monika Hicks, LCSW.

Does Monika offer Christian or faith-integrated therapy?

Yes, when a client prefers it. Monika Hicks, LCSW can integrate a Christian, faith-based approach into therapy, and faith is never imposed on anyone who does not want it. She practises as a licensed clinical social worker, not as a pastor, so therapy sits alongside your church life rather than replacing it.

Can I see Monika if I live in Nashville, Knoxville or elsewhere in Tennessee?

Yes. Therapy with Monika is online, so clients located anywhere in Tennessee during their sessions can book, from Jackson and Nashville to Chattanooga and Knoxville. The Memphis focus of this page describes where many clients live, not a limit on who can be seen in the state.

What if I live in Southaven, Olive Branch or West Memphis?

Therapy depends on where you are physically located during each session. Sessions joined from Mississippi or Arkansas are outside a Tennessee licence, so they are not available. Joining from a Tennessee location, such as your Memphis workplace, can work. Coaching, which is non-clinical, is not limited by state in the same way.

What therapy approach does Monika use?

Monika's practice draws on Solution Focused Therapy, which keeps attention on your strengths, your goals and the next workable step rather than only on what has gone wrong. Sessions remain individual, so the pace and focus follow what you bring, whether that is anxiety, burnout, grief or a life transition.
