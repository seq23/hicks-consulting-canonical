Frequently asked questions

Do I need to live in Cordova, Tennessee to participate?

You must be physically located anywhere within the state of Tennessee during your virtual session. Services are delivered entirely online with no office visits required, welcoming adults from Cordova and surrounding communities.

What is the difference between therapy and coaching?

Virtual individual therapy focuses on clinical mental health concerns and emotional healing. Virtual coaching is non-clinical and focuses on personal goals, skill-building, and forward-looking action plans rather than treating mental health symptoms.

Can I request a faith-integrated approach?

You can request a Christian, faith-integrated approach in either your therapy sessions or your coaching sessions. This approach is integrated only when you specifically prefer it and is never imposed upon you by the practitioner.

What should I do if I am in an immediate crisis?

Anyone who is experiencing an immediate crisis should call or text 988 immediately to connect with the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline for free and confidential support available twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.
