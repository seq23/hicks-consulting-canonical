Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between therapy and coaching?

Therapy is clinical mental health treatment focused on healing, processing emotions, and addressing conditions like anxiety. Coaching is non-clinical and forward-looking, focusing on personal growth, actionable goals, and building practical habits for your daily life without diagnosing or treating mental health disorders.

How do virtual sessions work for adults in Tennessee?

Virtual sessions take place securely online via video from any private location in Tennessee. You connect using your computer, tablet, or smartphone. This format removes travel time, making it easier to fit mental health support into your busy weekly schedule.

What should I expect during a support group?

A support group brings together adults facing similar life experiences, stress, or transitions to share insights and encouragement. It provides a confidential, community-focused space where you can speak openly, listen to others, and realize you do not navigate your challenges alone.

How do I know if I need mental health support?

You might need support if you feel persistently overwhelmed, emotionally exhausted, or stuck in repeating cycles of anxiety and burnout. When coping strategies you usually rely on stop working, seeking professional guidance offers a grounded way to process your experience.
