Solution Focused Therapy in Memphis, TN

Solution focused therapy is a goal-oriented approach to mental health that emphasizes your strengths and future possibilities rather than past problems. It is a collaborative process where the focus remains on identifying small, manageable steps toward the life you want to lead. This method is often used for managing stress, burnout, and relationship strain.

Published 3 November 2026 · By Monika Hicks, LCSW

How does solution focused therapy work for Memphis adults?

Solution focused therapy operates on the idea that you already possess the tools needed for change. Instead of spending long periods analyzing past traumas, this approach looks at what is working in your life right now. It asks how your life would look if the current problem were solved. This can be helpful for Black women in Memphis balancing the demands of career, family, and community. Whether you are navigating traffic on Poplar Avenue or managing a busy household in Whitehaven or Cordova, this method respects your time. It focuses on practical shifts in perspective.

Is this approach right for burnout and life transitions?

Burnout often feels like a heavy weight that makes daily tasks seem impossible. Life transitions, such as a new job or a change in relationship status, can also cause significant strain. A solution-focused approach helps by breaking down these large stressors into smaller parts. You look for 'exceptions'—times when the problem was not present or was less intense. This helps identify what you did differently during those times. It is a grounded way to address anxiety without feeling overwhelmed by the history of the issue.

What can I expect during a virtual session?

Virtual therapy sessions are conducted through a secure video platform. You should be in a private, quiet location within the state of Tennessee during the call. The conversation is centered on your current needs and future hopes. If you prefer a Christian, faith-integrated approach, this can be included in your therapy or coaching. This is only done when you request it and is never imposed. It allows you to bring your whole self, including your faith, into the room if that is important to you.

The structure of these sessions is designed to be clear and direct. You might discuss your vision for the next week or month. You might identify one small action that aligns with that vision. This approach is about building momentum. It is not about finding a quick fix but about recognizing your own agency. The goal is to help you see the resources you already have available to you.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between therapy and coaching?

Therapy is a clinical service designed to treat mental health conditions and address emotional distress. Coaching is a non-clinical service focused on specific life goals, performance, or transitions. Hicks Consulting offers both as separate virtual services for adults in Tennessee.

Can I include my faith in my sessions?

Yes. A Christian, faith-integrated approach can be used in therapy or coaching if you prefer it. This is never forced on any client. It is an option for those who want their spiritual beliefs to be part of their growth process.

Do I need to go to an office in Memphis?

No. All services at Hicks Consulting are virtual. There is no physical office for visits. You must be located in the state of Tennessee at the time of your appointment to receive therapy services.

What should I do if I am in a mental health crisis?

If you are in a crisis or experiencing an emergency, please call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. You can also contact the Tennessee Statewide Crisis Line or go to the nearest emergency room.
