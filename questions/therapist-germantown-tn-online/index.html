What is the difference between therapy, coaching, and support groups?

Deciding which service fits your needs depends on your current goals and what kind of support you are looking for. Therapy is a clinical service focused on mental health, emotional healing, and working through deep-seated patterns of anxiety, stress, or trauma. It is designed for adults in Tennessee who want a clinical professional to help them understand and manage emotional health challenges. Therapy never involves medical, legal, or financial advice, and it does not promise specific outcomes.

Coaching is a non-clinical service that is not therapy. Coaching focuses on practical goal setting, personal growth, accountability, and navigating specific current circumstances rather than clinical mental health treatment. Support groups offer a community setting where adults facing similar life transitions or stressors can share experiences and gain mutual encouragement in a guided format.
