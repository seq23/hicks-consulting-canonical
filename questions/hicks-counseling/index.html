What Is Hicks Consulting and How Can It Support You?

Hicks counseling and consulting is a professional practice offering virtual individual therapy for adults in Tennessee, non-clinical coaching, support groups, and organizational mental health training. The practice helps individuals navigate stress, burnout, anxiety, life transitions, and relationship strain while also providing expert workplace mental health education for employers across the state.

Published 3 November 2026 · Hicks Consulting

Are you carrying heavy stress or burnout right now?

Life often brings heavy demands that take a toll on your mind and body. You might feel constant exhaustion from balancing work, family, and personal expectations. This kind of deep stress can leave you feeling depleted and disconnected from yourself. Black women often carry unique layers of pressure in professional and personal spaces. You may feel an unspoken expectation to stay strong for everyone else. Over time, this constant weight can turn into chronic burnout and quiet anxiety. Acknowledging that you need a supportive space is a powerful first step toward relief.

Reading about mental health and wellness is helpful, but it is not therapy. Articles and self-help books cannot replace a dedicated space to process your specific experiences. When life transitions or relationship strain catch up with you, you deserve personalized attention. You do not have to figure everything out on your own. Exploring professional support can help you untangle complex feelings without judgment. If you are experiencing a crisis, please call or text 988 immediately to connect with someone who can help right away.

How do virtual therapy and coaching differ?

Virtual individual therapy provides a clinical space for adults living anywhere in Tennessee. Therapy focuses on deep emotional healing, understanding past patterns, and managing mental health conditions like anxiety. Your sessions happen securely online from the comfort of your own home or private space. This format removes the travel barrier and fits more easily into a busy schedule. You can explore difficult emotions, process grief, and build coping strategies with professional guidance.

Coaching offers a different path for personal and professional growth. Coaching is non-clinical and is not therapy. Instead of focusing on mental health diagnoses or deep-seated trauma, coaching looks forward. It helps you set concrete goals, overcome mental blocks, and navigate career or life changes. If you want practical strategies for accountability and personal development without clinical treatment, coaching may be the right fit. You can learn more about these options on the Hicks Consulting page.

Could a support group or organizational training help?

Support groups bring people together who share similar life experiences and challenges. Being in a room, even a virtual one, with others who truly understand your perspective can reduce feelings of isolation. Support groups offer a shared space to listen, learn, and grow alongside peers. You gain strength from community wisdom while sharing your own insights at your own pace. This collective environment fosters deep connection and practical encouragement for everyday life.

Employers also play a vital role in fostering healthy environments. Hicks Consulting provides organizational mental health training and speaking engagements for workplaces. These programs help teams recognize signs of burnout, build supportive cultures, and prioritize well-being on the job. Healthy workplaces benefit both employees and the broader community. You can review the About Monika Hicks page to learn more about the foundation of the practice.

What should you consider before reaching out?

Deciding to seek professional support is a deeply personal choice. You might wonder whether therapy, coaching, or a support group best fits your current needs. Take time to reflect on what you are experiencing right now. Are you dealing with specific symptoms of anxiety or navigating a difficult relationship? Or are you facing a major life transition that requires forward-looking strategy and guidance?

Every person's journey is entirely unique. There is no single correct path, and you can explore different avenues over time. Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between therapy and coaching?

Therapy is a clinical service focused on mental health, emotional healing, and diagnosing or treating conditions like anxiety. Coaching is strictly non-clinical, focusing on future goals, personal growth, skill-building, and accountability without treating mental health disorders.

Are sessions held online or in person?

All individual therapy sessions, coaching sessions, and support groups are conducted virtually. This allows adults living anywhere in the state of Tennessee to access professional support from a private, comfortable location of their choice.

What does workplace mental health training cover?

Organizational mental health training and speaking engagements focus on helping employers and teams recognize burnout, reduce workplace stress, and foster supportive environments. These programs are tailored to educate companies on prioritizing employee well-being.

How do I know which service to choose?

Choosing between therapy, coaching, or a support group depends on your current goals and needs. If you are dealing with anxiety or emotional distress, therapy is appropriate. If you want goal-oriented guidance, coaching is a better fit.
