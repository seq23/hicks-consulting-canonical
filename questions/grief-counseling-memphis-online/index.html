Grief Counseling in Memphis: Online Support Options

Grief counseling is a type of therapy designed to help adults process the emotional and physical impact of loss. In Memphis, this support is available through virtual individual therapy and support groups. These services focus on navigating the complex feelings that follow the death of a loved one or other significant life changes.

Published 3 November 2026 · By Monika Hicks, LCSW

How does online grief counseling work in Memphis?

Online grief counseling allows Memphis residents to meet with a licensed professional from their own homes. This service is provided through secure video platforms. It is available to adults located anywhere in Tennessee during their session time. This includes neighborhoods like Whitehaven, Orange Mound, and Cordova. You do not need to commute through traffic on I-240 or Poplar Avenue to attend a session. Reading about grief is helpful, but it is not a substitute for professional therapy. If you are in an immediate crisis, please call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.

Virtual sessions offer a private space to discuss the weight of loss. Many people find that being in a familiar environment helps them speak more freely. The process involves talking through the stages of grief and identifying how loss affects your daily life. It is a grounded way to address burnout and emotional strain. For those who prefer a non-clinical approach, virtual coaching is also an option. Coaching focuses on future goals and navigating life transitions rather than clinical mental health treatment.

What are the benefits of a faith-integrated approach?

For many in the Memphis community, faith is a central part of the healing process. Hicks Consulting offers a Christian, faith-integrated approach for those who want it. This means your spiritual beliefs can be part of the conversation about loss and hope. This approach is never imposed on any client. It is only included if you specifically request it. A licensed clinical social worker facilitates these sessions with a focus on your personal values.

Integrating faith can provide a sense of grounding during a difficult life transition. It allows you to explore your grief within the context of your spiritual framework. This can be particularly meaningful for Black women who rely on their faith community for strength. Whether you are looking for traditional therapy or a faith-based perspective, the goal is to provide a supportive environment for your journey.

  • Individual therapy for deep emotional processing.
  • Support groups for connecting with others facing loss.
  • Faith-integrated sessions for spiritual alignment.
  • Virtual coaching for navigating life changes.
  • Workplace training for employers supporting grieving staff.

Are there local resources for grief in Shelby County?

Memphis offers several community resources for those experiencing loss. The Shelby County Government provides information on community mental health services. Local organizations often host grief workshops near landmarks like Overton Park or the Benjamin L. Hooks Central Library. While Hicks Consulting provides online-only services, knowing your local landscape is important. You can find more information on how online therapy works in Memphis on our dedicated page.

In addition to private therapy, community-based support can be a vital part of your network. Organizations like the Memphis Crisis Center provide 24-hour support for those in emotional distress. These resources are available to all residents of the Mid-South. Accessing help is a practical step toward managing the strain of grief. Remember that professional support is a tool for navigating these difficult times, not a quick fix for the pain of loss.

What should you expect in a grief support group?

Support groups bring together people who are experiencing similar types of loss. These groups are facilitated to ensure a safe and respectful environment. In a group setting, you can hear from others who understand the unique challenges of grief. This can reduce the feeling of isolation that often follows a death or major life change. Groups at Hicks Consulting are conducted virtually for residents across Tennessee.

Participating in a group allows for shared learning and mutual support. It is a space where your experiences are validated by peers. The focus is on healthy coping strategies and communal healing. For employers, organizational mental health training can also address how to support a team through collective grief. This helps create a more supportive workplace culture in Memphis businesses.

Frequently asked questions

Is online grief counseling as effective as in-person?

Many people find online sessions a workable way to talk about loss. It offers convenience for those with busy schedules in Memphis. Many find the virtual format reduces the stress of travel. It allows you to receive support in a comfortable, private setting while located in Tennessee.

Can I choose a Christian perspective for my grief sessions?

Yes, a Christian, faith-integrated approach is available upon request. This is never forced on any client. It is a choice for those who want their faith to be a part of their healing process. The therapist is a licensed clinical social worker who respects your preferences.

What is the difference between grief therapy and coaching?

Grief therapy is a clinical service for processing deep emotional pain and mental health concerns. Coaching is a non-clinical service focused on navigating life transitions and setting future goals. Both are available virtually to adults in Tennessee. The right choice depends on your specific needs.

How can I find immediate help in Memphis?

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. These services are free, confidential, and available 24 hours a day for anyone in the Shelby County area.
