What is the difference between therapy and coaching?

It is important to know which service fits your current needs. Therapy is a clinical service designed to treat mental health conditions. It focuses on healing from past wounds and managing symptoms of anxiety or depression. Licensed professionals conduct these sessions. Coaching is non-clinical and does not involve therapy. It is more focused on reaching specific life goals or improving performance. Coaching helps you look forward and create actionable plans. Hicks Consulting offers both virtual individual therapy and virtual coaching. This allows you to choose the path that aligns with your goals. Some people start with therapy to address deep-seated stress. Others prefer coaching to navigate a specific career change or life transition. Both options provide professional guidance and support. Reading this information is not a substitute for professional therapy. If you are unsure which service you need, a consultation can help clarify your options. Understanding these definitions ensures you receive the right type of care for your situation.
